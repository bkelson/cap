# Vision and principles

## Vision

**Make the costs of AI visible, so using it responsibly becomes a habit.**

## Mission

Cap is a dependable, no-frills way to see your Claude usage, where you want it and the way you like it.

## Principles

**1. Dependable first.** Cap works quietly, or tells you plainly why it can't.

**2. Privacy.** Cap tells you what it reads and what it sends, in plain language. Anything beyond today's core is off until you choose it, and you can turn it off at any time. Cap never asks for your Claude password or login token.

**3. Transparency.** Every figure shows where it comes from. Estimates are labelled as estimates, with their method visible, and gaps are admitted.

**4. Light on resources.** Cap spends as little compute as it can, pauses when you're away, and keeps its own cost negligible: each refresh is a very small request, so you never have to think about it.

**5. Simplicity.** Cap is a supporting player. It takes little space and steps aside, so your work stays the main thing. It offers a choice only where it truly helps. If Cap ever pulls focus for itself, we've got it wrong.

**6. Open source, always.** Cap's code is free to read, build and improve, and it stays that way. If you have a fix, an idea or a better way, you're warmly invited to help make Cap as good as it can be for the Claude community.

## Where this is going

Cap starts with the core: a stable app that reports your usage dependably. Everything beyond that, including any estimate of energy or water, only arrives with a method that can be defended and shown. See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

Cap is an independent project and is not affiliated with Anthropic.
